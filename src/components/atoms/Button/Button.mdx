# Button

<Localized
  en={
    <>
      <p>
        The <strong>Button</strong> atom is the primary interactive control. It consumes
        semantic <code>var(--…)</code> tokens only, so it reskins across the light/dark
        theme axis and the neutral/pixel skin axis without any change to its own CSS. Its
        label and its loading announcement are bilingual through the typed{' '}
        <code>t()</code> indirection.
      </p>
      <p>
        Use <code>intent</code> for meaning (<code>primary</code>, <code>neutral</code>,{' '}
        <code>success</code>, <code>warning</code>, <code>info</code>, <code>danger</code>
        ), <code>variantSize</code> for scale, and <code>pending</code> to show a busy
        state — a pending button is disabled and exposes <code>aria-busy</code> plus a
        screen-reader-only status. Native <code>&lt;button&gt;</code> attributes pass
        through unchanged; a prop never reuses a native name with another meaning (ADR
        0015).
      </p>
      <p>
        The status intents <code>success</code>, <code>warning</code>, <code>info</code>{' '}
        and <code>danger</code> use the global status tokens{' '}
        <code>--omnia-color-&lt;status&gt;</code> with their <code>-hover</code>/
        <code>-active</code> shades and <code>--omnia-color-text-on-&lt;status&gt;</code>{' '}
        (ADR 0012). Every intent reads its rest, hover and press colours from the shared
        intent map (<code>--omnia-intent-bg</code>, <code>--omnia-intent-bg-hover</code>,{' '}
        <code>--omnia-intent-bg-active</code>, <code>--omnia-intent-fg</code>).
      </p>
      <p>
        On hover the button lifts and its offset shadow grows; on press it collapses onto
        the page (see ADR 0009). All motion respects <code>prefers-reduced-motion</code>,
        falling back to colour and shadow cues so states stay distinguishable without
        movement.
      </p>
    </>
  }

        0012). Chaque intention lit ses couleurs de repos, de survol et d'enfoncement dans
